# Galuxium Nexus V2 — Product & Domain Specification

## Product Identity
- **Product Name:** Galuxium Nexus V2
- **Tagline:** Autonomous AI Invoice Reconciliation & GST Compliance Engine for Indian SMEs & Accounting Practices
- **Codename / Slug:** `galuxium-nexus-v2`
- **Target Audience:** Indian Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs), Chartered Accountants (CAs), GST Practitioners, and In-house Finance Teams.
- **Problem Statement:** Indian businesses spend an average of 42 hours each month manually matching supplier invoices against bank debits and GSTR-2B filing returns, risking ITC (Input Tax Credit) loss, GST notices, and cash leakage.
- **Solution:** An end-to-end autonomous reconciliation pipeline powered by multimodal vision AI, deterministic GST arithmetic auditing, fuzzy heuristic matching, and a cryptographic tamper-evident audit ledger.

## Domain & Hosting Identity
- **Vercel Project Name:** `galuxium-nexus-v2`

- **Apex Domain Target:** `galuxium.app`
- **Region:** `bom1` (Mumbai, India) for minimal latency with Indian banking and tax portal APIs.
